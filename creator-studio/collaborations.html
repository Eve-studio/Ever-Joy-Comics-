HOW IT WORKS

Collaboration, without the chaos.

⌕
Discover

Browse creators who are currently available and filter by role, genre, and creative skills.

✉
Request

Submit a structured collaboration request explaining your project and what you need.

✓
Match

Once the creator accepts, the collaboration becomes active and their availability automatically turns off.

⚖
One active collaboration at a time.

Ever Joy automatically removes a creator from the available pool when they accept a collaboration. This keeps opportunities fair and prevents creators from collecting multiple projects while others are still waiting for a chance.

✦
No public posts. No comment sections. No complicated chat.

Collaboration on Ever Joy is intentionally structured. Your profile tells creators what you do, the request form explains the project, and both creators can decide whether the opportunity is right for them.
