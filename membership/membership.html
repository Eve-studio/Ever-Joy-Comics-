HOW READING WORKS

Read freely. Unlock when it matters.

Free readers can enjoy the early part of comics with advertisements. Paid unlocking begins later in a work, giving readers time to discover a story while ensuring creators can earn from longer-running series.

First 18 chapters Free readers can read with ads. Later chapters Ever Gems are used to unlock paid chapters.
